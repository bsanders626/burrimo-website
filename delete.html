Request Account & Data Deletion – Burrimo

If you would like to delete your Burrimo account and associated data, please contact us using the email below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@burrimo.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account
  3. Include the email address associated with your account

What will be deleted

  • Your account information (email, username, profile)
  • Uploaded photos
  • Posts, ratings, hearts, bookmarks, and comments
  • Saved restaurants and preferences

We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

If you have questions, contact support@burrimo.com.
